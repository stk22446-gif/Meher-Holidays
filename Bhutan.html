Frequently Asked Questions

Your right to Know!

Q: Do Indian tourists need a passport or visa to visit Bhutan?

A: Indian citizens do not require a visa to visit Bhutan. However, you must carry either a valid original Passport (with at least 6 months validity) or an original Voter ID Card issued by the Election Commission of India. An entry permit will be issued at the border.

Q: What is the Sustainable Development Fee (SDF) for Bhutan?

A: Indian tourists are required to pay a Sustainable Development Fee (SDF) of INR 1,200/- per person per night. For other international nationalities, the fee is USD 100/- per person per night.

Q: Is the trek to Tiger's Nest difficult?

A: The trek to Taktsang (Tiger's Nest) Monastery is moderately challenging and takes about 4 to 5 hours round trip. It is a steady uphill climb, but horses can be hired (for going uphill only) if needed. We recommend regular walking/cardio preparation before the trip.

Q: Can I use Indian Rupees (INR) in Bhutan?

A: Yes, Indian Rupees in denominations of INR 100 and INR 500 are widely accepted across Bhutan. However, we advise carrying physical cash, as digital transactions and cards might not work in remote locations.

Q: What is the best time of year to visit Bhutan?

A: The best time to visit Bhutan is from October to December (clear sunny skies and pleasant weather) and from March to May (spring season with beautiful blooming flowers).
