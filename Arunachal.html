Frequently Asked Questions

Your right to Know!

Q: How much does a trip to Arunachal Pradesh cost?

A: A standard 7-8 day trip can cost between ₹25,000 to ₹40,000 per person depending on group size and stay preferences.

Q: Is an Inner Line Permit (ILP) mandatory?

A: Yes, all Indian and foreign tourists require a permit to enter Arunachal Pradesh. We can assist you with the process.

Q: What is the best time to visit?

A: October to April is the peak season. March-April is great for blossoms, while winters are perfect for snow in Tawang.

Q: What are the must-try local dishes?

A: Don't miss Thukpa, Momos, and the traditional bamboo shoot delicacies like Pehak.

Q: Is Arunachal safe for solo travelers?

A: Arunachal is extremely safe and welcoming. Locals are known for their hospitality and helpful nature.
