Best Time to Visit:
  • June to September is the ideal time to explore the Zanskar–Ladakh region.
  • The Manali–Leh Highway and Shinku La route are generally accessible during this period (subject to weather conditions).
  • July and August offer pleasant daytime temperatures and clear mountain views.
  • September is perfect for photography, stargazing, and experiencing vibrant autumn landscapes.
  • High-altitude regions such as Hanle, Pangong, and Nubra Valley can remain cold even during summer, especially at night.
  • Winter travel (October–May) is not recommended due to heavy snowfall, road closures, and extreme weather conditions.
Connectivity:
  • Mobile connectivity is limited in many remote areas.
  • Only postpaid SIM cards work in Ladakh; prepaid numbers (including those from other states) do not work here.
  • Wi-Fi is available only in Leh town and at a few campsites/hotels; mobile data services are extremely slow or unavailable in most parts of Ladakh.
  • Carry offline maps, essential contacts, and emergency numbers as digital connectivity is unreliable.
  • In case of emergency, contact the local police or tourism helpline numbers displayed at guesthouses and hotels.
  • Network coverage is extremely limited or unavailable in Padum, Hanle, and remote stretches.
Altitude & Acclimatization:
  • This route passes through very high-altitude regions (10,000–15,000+ ft).
  • Avoid alcohol and smoking for the first 24–48 hours.
  • Inform your guide immediately about any symptoms of altitude sickness such as headache, nausea, dizziness, or breathing difficulty.
  • Avoid overexertion during the initial days and take gradual acclimatization walks.
Weather:
  • The weather in Ladakh is unpredictable and can change rapidly.
  • Pack warm layers, including insulated jackets, fleece, thermal wear, woollen gloves, and a beanie, even during summer months.
  • Carry rain gear such as a waterproof jacket and trousers, as rainfall can occur unexpectedly.
  • Sun protection is essential, even on cloudy days, due to the high altitude and thin atmosphere.
  • Night temperatures can drop significantly, often below freezing, even during summer.
Eco Tips:
  • Avoid single-use plastics.
  • Carry reusable water bottles.
  • Follow designated trails while trekking and avoid stepping on fragile alpine vegetation.
  • Do not disturb wildlife; maintain a safe distance and avoid feeding animals.
  • Carry snacks such as energy bars, nuts and dried fruits for long journeys.
