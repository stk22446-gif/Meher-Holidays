Why Beyond the Monasteries: Tawang Experience?

The Beyond the Monasteries: Tawang Experience is a thrilling journey through the breathtaking landscapes of Guwahati, Dirang, Tawang, and Bumla Pass. Experience majestic mountains, ancient monasteries, scenic valleys, and unforgettable Himalayan views with Meher Holidays.


Discover the beauty and spiritual charm of Tawang on this specially curated tour.

Beyond the Monasteries: Tawang Experience Itinerary

Cost Per Person - INR 27,199/- including GST

ITINERARY (5 Nights / 6 Days)

Contact Us Download Itinerary
  • • Arrive at Guwahati in the morning and embark on a scenic drive to Dirang.
  • • En route, you will cross into Arunachal Pradesh at Bhalukpong, where your Inner Line Permit will be verified.
  • • Check in to your hotel on arrival.
  • • Overnight stay at Dirang
  • • After breakfast, proceed towards Tawang with enchanting stops along the way
    > Jaswant Garh War Memorial – Dedicated to the brave soldier Jaswant Singh Rawat of the 1962 Sino-Indian War.
    > Sela Pass (13,700 ft) – Snow-clad and breathtaking, one of the highest motorable passes in the world.
    > Nuranang Falls (Jang Falls) – Among the most spectacular waterfalls in Arunachal Pradesh.
  • • Arrive in Tawang and check in to your hotel.
  • • Overnight stay at Tawang
  • • Travel by local cab on direct payment basis
  • • Post breakfast, embark on a full-day excursion around Tawang:
    > Bum La Pass (16,500 ft) – Located on the Indo-China border, steeped in history from the 1962 war, and believed to be the entry route of the Dalai Lama into India. A surreal, often snow-covered landscape.
    > Sangetsar Lake (Madhuri Lake) – A high-altitude glacial lake formed after the 1950 earthquake, famous for its raw beauty and spiritual significance.
    > Pangateng Tso Lake (P.T. Tso) – A pristine glacial lake surrounded by mountains, 17 km from Tawang.
  • • Return to Tawang by evening.
  • • After breakfast, explore key landmarks of Tawang:
    > Tawang Monastery (Gaden Namgyal Lhatse) – One of the largest Buddhist monasteries in the world, established in the 17th century by Mera Lama Monpa a 400-year-old monastery, second largest in India, home to 700+ monks.
    > Urqelling Monastery – Birthplace of the 6th Dalai Lama, offering panoramic views of the surrounding valley.
    > Tawang War Memorial – A magnificent memorial dedicated to the soldiers who sacrificed their lives during the 1962 Sino-Indian War.
  • • Later drive to Bomdila and check in to your hotel.
  • • Overnight stay at Bomdila
  • • After breakfast, visit Bomdila Monastery, a serene center of Buddhism with spectacular Himalayan views, including Kangto and Gorichen peaks.
  • • Then, drive back to Guwahati. On arrival, check in at the hotel and relax.
  • • Overnight stay at Guwahati
  • • After breakfast, if time permits, visit the sacred Maa Kamakhya Temple, one of the most important Shakti Peethas in India.
  • • Later, transfer to Guwahati Airport / Railway Station for your onward journey.

Other Information

Must Carry

  • Authentic Government ID Card (mandatory)
  • Inner Line Permit (ILP) copy for Arunachal Pradesh
  • Warm layered clothing for high-altitude regions
  • Heavy woolen/down jacket
  • Sunglasses with UV protection
  • Sunscreen (SPF 50+) and lip balm
  • Personal medicines and basic first-aid kit
  • Mobile charger and power bank
  • Reusable water bottle
  • Cash for local purchases and remote areas
  • Raincoat or poncho for sudden weather changes
Best Time to Visit:
  • March to May and September to November for clear skies, pleasant weather, and stunning Himalayan views.
Connectivity:
  • BSNL, Airtel, and Jio work in major towns like Dirang and Tawang, but connectivity may be limited around Bumla Pass and remote stretches.
Weather:
  • Winters (November–February) are cold with heavy snowfall in higher reaches.
  • Summers (March–May) are pleasant with mild temperatures.
  • Monsoons (June–September) bring heavy rainfall, landslides, and road closures, making travel challenging.
Altitude Tips:
  • Tawang and Bumla Pass are located at high altitudes. Stay hydrated, avoid overexertion, and allow time for proper acclimatization.
Eco Tips:
  • Respect local monasteries and traditions.
  • Do not pick wildflowers or disturb wildlife.
  • Avoid littering.
  • Carry reusable water bottles.
  • Avoid campfire and use gas stoves for cooking.
  • Help preserve the pristine beauty of Arunachal Pradesh.
