Why Majestic Meadows of Kashmir?

The Majestic Meadows of Kashmir takes you through the breathtaking beauty of Srinagar, Uri, Guwaas, and Tosamaidan. From serene lakes and lush meadows to majestic mountains and untouched valleys, every destination offers a unique and unforgettable experience.


Join Meher Holidays and discover the true magic of Kashmir on this specially curated adventure.

Majestic Meadows of Kashmir Itinerary

Cost Per Person - INR 20,999/- including GST

ITINERARY (5 Nights / 6 Days)

Contact Us Download Itinerary
  • • Arrival at Srinagar Airport:
    > Begin your Kashmiri adventure with a warm welcome from our representatives, who will escort you to your hotel.
  • • Mughal Gardens Exploration:
    > Immerse yourself in the beauty of Srinagar's famed Mughal Gardens, including the vibrant Shalimar Garden, historic Nishat Bagh, the majestic Pari Mahal, and the serene Chashme Shahi.
  • • Visit the revered Shankaracharya Temple, offering panoramic views of the valley.
  • • Dal Lake Shikara Ride:
    > Enjoy a complimentary 1-hour Shikara ride on Dal Lake, gliding past houseboats and picturesque landscapes.
  • • Overnight stay at Srinagar
  • • Embark on a scenic drive to Uri (approx. 3-4 hours), traversing picturesque valleys and mountain passes.
  • • Step into a region of historical significance:
    > Kaman Post (Aman Setu - LoC Visit): Witness the unique landscape of the Line of Control, a poignant reminder of the region's complex history.
    > (Please ensure you carry your original physical ID cards for necessary permits.)
  • • Uri Hydroelectric Power Project:
    > Learn about the vital role this project plays in the region's development.
  • • Uri Heritage Sites:
    > Discover the historical importance of the area.
  • • Eco Park (Zin Line Adventure Activities):
    > Choose from optional activities like zip-lining or nature walks, immersing yourself in the pristine environment.
  • • Nambla Waterfall:
    > Discover this hidden gem, where cascading waters create a mesmerizing spectacle.
  • • Tea and Snacks at Iqbal Resort:
    > Relax and enjoy the scenic views.
  • • Experience the warmth of Kashmiri hospitality at a local homestay in Uri. Savor an authentic Kashmiri dinner (veg/non-veg), prepared with local ingredients and traditional recipes.
  • • Overnight Stay: Homestay in Uri
  • • Ascend to the breathtaking heights of:
    > Sirthani Top: Capture panoramic views of the majestic Pir Panjal range, a sight that will leave you spellbound.
    > Mini Gulmarg: Explore this serene meadow, a hidden paradise perfect for photography and quiet contemplation.
  • • Immerse yourself in the authentic charm of:
    > Dudran Village: Experience the traditional way of life in a Kashmiri village, interacting with locals and learning about their customs.
  • • Embark on an unforgettable camping experience at the enchanting Guwaas:
    > A hidden gem with lush green meadows and breathtaking landscapes, perfect for camping under the stars.
  • • Enjoy a bonfire, stargazing, and authentic Kashmiri hospitality.
  • • Overnight Stay: Tent stay surrounded by the tranquility of nature.
  • • Enjoy the crisp mountain air, and a morning trek around the beautiful Guwaas meadows.
  • • Bid farewell to the serene landscapes of Guwaas and journey back to Srinagar.
  • • Enjoy a relaxing evening in Srinagar. perhaps a shikara ride on Dal Lake, or a visit to a local craft market.
  • • Overnight Stay: Srinagar
  • • Explore the captivating beauty of Tosa Maidan, the "King of Meadows."
  • • Witness the expansive, lush green meadows, surrounded by dense forests of deodar, pine, and fir trees.
  • • Marvel at the breathtaking views of the snow-capped Pir Panjal mountains.
  • • Enjoy a packed lunch amidst the serene surroundings.
  • • Return to Srinagar, reflecting on the day's unforgettable experience.
  • • Overnight Stay: Srinagar
  • • After a leisurely breakfast, bid farewell to the enchanting landscapes of Kashmir.
  • • Transfer: Proceed to Srinagar Airport (SXR) for your onward journey, carrying with you cherished memories of your extraordinary adventure through the border trails and majestic meadows of Kashmir.

Other Information

Must Carry

  • Authentic Government ID Card
  • Warm layered clothing for cold weather in Tosamaidan
  • Heavy jacket / fleece jacket
  • Comfortable trekking shoes with good grip
  • Sunglasses with UV protection
  • Sunscreen (SPF 30+) and lip balm
  • Personal medicines and basic first-aid kit
  • Mobile charger and power bank
  • Reusable water bottle
  • Raincoat / poncho for sudden weather changes
  • Cash for remote areas with limited digital payment facilities
Best Time to Visit:
  • Summer (April to June): Pleasant weather, sightseeing, and outdoor activities
  • Autumn (September to November): Beautiful fall colors, peaceful atmosphere
  • Winter (December to February): Snow sports, winter landscapes, a true winter wonderland
  • Spring (March to May): Blooming flowers, pleasant weather, and outdoor activities
Connectivity:
  • Jio, Airtel and BSNL work in Srinagar and Uri, but network connectivity may be weak or unavailable in Guwaas and Tosamaidan.
Weather:
  • Tosamaidan remains cool even during summer, while mornings and evenings can be cold
  • Carry warm layered clothing throughout the trip
Adventure Activities:
  • Camping, trekking, meadow walks, horse riding
  • Photography tours are popular activities in the Tosamaidan region
Eco Tips:
  • Avoid littering
  • Avoid plastic and carry reusable water bottles
  • Respect wildlife and natural surroundings
  • Help preserve the untouched beauty of Kashmir’s meadows and forests

Travel Essentials

Gears:
  • Rucksack bag / travel backpack
  • Day pack for sightseeing and meadow walks
  • Reusable water bottle
  • Mobile charger and power bank
  • Camera and extra batteries/memory cards (optional)
  • Torch / LED flashlight
Clothes:
  • Thermal innerwear (recommended during colder months)
  • 2–3 comfortable T-shirts or shirts
  • 1–2 full-sleeve T-shirts
  • 1 fleece jacket
  • 1 heavy woolen/down jacket
  • Comfortable trekking pants or cargo pants
  • Woolen cap / beanie
  • Warm gloves
  • 3–4 pairs of socks (including woolen socks)
  • Undergarments as required
  • Rain jacket or poncho
  • Nightwear for hotel stay
Footwear:
  • Comfortable trekking shoes with good grip
  • Waterproof shoes (recommended during monsoon/winter)
  • Flip-flops / sandals for hotel stay
  • Extra pair of socks for wet/cold conditions
Medication:
  • Personal prescription medicines
  • Motion sickness tablets
  • Headache and fever medication
  • ORS / electrolyte sachets
  • Basic first-aid kit
  • Bandages and antiseptic cream
  • Medicines for cold, cough, and stomach upset
Personal Accessories:
  • Toothbrush and toothpaste
  • Sanitizer and wet wipes
  • Sunscreen and lip balm
  • Moisturizer and cold cream
  • Sunglasses and cap
  • Small towel and toiletries
  • Travel documents and ID proof copies
