Why Palace to Dunes Experience?

The Palace to Dunes Experience tour is a fascinating journey through the vibrant cities of Jaipur and Jaisalmer. Experience magnificent palaces, historic forts, colorful bazaars, and the enchanting golden sand dunes of the Thar Desert with Meher Holidays.

Discover the royal heritage and desert charm of Rajasthan on this specially curated tour.

Palace to Dunes Experience Itinerary

Cost Per Person - INR 21,999/- including GST

ITINERARY (5 Nights / 6 Days)

Contact Us Download Itinerary
  • • Reach Jaipur airport/railway station and transfer to hotel
  • • By the evening we visit the modern attractions of the pink city, which includes the, Statue Circle, Birla Auditorium & Planetarium, New Vidhan Sabha, Birla Mandir and Jawahar Kala Kendra.
  • • From there we go for Rajasthani culinary experience with folk music and dance at ethnic village.
  • • Overnight stay in Jaipur
  • • After breakfast we drive out to explore the well planned city of Sawai Jai Singh II still alive with Colour, Joy, Regality, People, Art and Culture.
  • • Visit the busy Bazaars, the Hawamahal, City Palace and Jantar Mantar.
  • • After lunch we visit Amber Fort & Palace, Jagat Shiromani Temple and pass through the scenic Jal Mahal.
  • • Overnight stay in Jaipur
  • • Leave Jaipur for Jodhpur, upon arrival check in at hotel.
  • • Soon we climb up the Majestic Mehrangarh Fort and explore its every nook & corner.
  • • We also visit the Jaswant Thada, Ummaid Bhawan Palace Museum, Clock Tower and Markets.
  • • Overnight stay in Jodhpur
  • • After breakfast we proceed to Jaisalmer.
  • • On the way to Jaisalmer we make short halt at Kheechan to see thousand of migratory birds.
  • • Reach Jaisalmer by afternoon.
  • • Upon arrival check in at hotel.
  • • In evening we visit Gadisar Lake & Vyas Chhatri.
  • • Dinner and overnight stay in Jaisalmer
  • • After breakfast we visit Jaisalmer Fort, Jain Temple & Patwa Haveli.
  • • By the evening we go for a unique experience of the Desert alike Arabian night by making overnight stay over sand dunes with tented accommodation with cultural program and also enjoy photogenic sunset on camel back.
  • • Overnight at Desert Camp.
  • • At the time of onwards journey, you will be transferred to Jaisalmer / Jodhpur Airport or Railway Station. (The Tour End with Sweet Memories)

Best Time to Visit:
  • October to March is the ideal time to visit Jaipur and Jaisalmer, with pleasant weather perfect for sightseeing and desert activities.
  • November to February offers cool temperatures, making it the best season for exploring forts, palaces, local markets, and desert camps.
  • December and January are especially popular for camel safaris, cultural performances, and overnight stays in the Thar Desert.
  • July to September brings occasional rainfall, adding a unique charm to Rajasthan’s landscapes and heritage sites.
  • April to June can be extremely hot, with daytime temperatures often exceeding 40°C, making outdoor sightseeing less comfortable.
Connectivity:
  • Network coverage varies in remote areas, particularly in the desert.
  • Most hotels and desert camps offer Wi-Fi, but speeds may be slower than in urban areas.
  • Jio, Airtel, Vi, and BSNL networks work well in Jaipur and Jaisalmer, including most tourist areas.
  • It is advisable to download offline maps and essential information before traveling to remote locations.
Weather:
  • Days can be warm while evenings in Jaisalmer's desert region may become cool, especially during winter months.
Eco Tips:
  • Respect local customs and traditions
  • Support local artisans and businesses
  • Avoid littering in heritage sites and desert areas
  • Carry reusable water bottles
  • Help preserve Rajasthan’s cultural and natural heritage.
