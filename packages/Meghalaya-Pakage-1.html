Why Abode of Clouds Escape?

The Abode of Clouds Escape tour is a captivating journey through the scenic landscapes of Shillong, Cherrapunjee, Kaziranga, and Guwahati. Experience rolling hills, majestic waterfalls, rich wildlife, and the vibrant culture of Northeast India with Meher Holidays.

Discover the natural beauty and unique charm of Meghalaya and Assam on this specially curated tour.

Adobe of Clouds Escape Itinerary

Cost Per Person - INR 39,699/- including GST

ITINERARY (8 Nights/ 9 Days)

Contact Us Download Itinerary
  • • Arrive at Guwahati Airport/Railway station (Assam).
  • • Transfer to Shillong (approx 100 km, 3 hours).
  • • Highlight enroute: Stop at Umiam Lake (Barapani) – a picturesque reservoir surrounded by pine-covered hills.
  • • Evening: Leisure walk & shopping at Police Bazar, Shillong’s bustling city center.
  • • Overnight stay at Shillong
  • • After breakfast, head towards the Jaintia Hills region:
    > Tyrchi Falls (short halt)
    > Krang Suri Falls – Turquoise pool with stunning views
    > Nartiang Monolith Park – Largest collection of monoliths in Meghalaya
    > Jowai Durga Temple – 500-year-old temple with historical significance
    > Phe Phe Falls – Scenic twin cascades amidst lush greenery
    > Laitlum Canyon – Breathtaking viewpoint known as the end of the world
  • • Overnight stay at Shillong
  • • After breakfast, visit the Mawphlang Sacred Grove, a mystical forest preserved by tribal traditions. (Local guide charges: Half trek ₹500 / Full trek ₹700 approx.)
  • • Continue journey towards Cherrapunjee (Sohra).
  • • Enroute sightseeing (time permitting):
    • Wahnienneng Viewpoint
    • Elephant Falls
    • Mawkdok Dympep Valley Viewpoint
    • Garden of Caves
  • • Overnight stay at Cherrapunjee
  • • After breakfast, enjoy a full day of exploration in Cherrapunjee:
  • > Ramakrishna Mission School & Museum – A renowned educational institution showcasing the history, culture, and spiritual heritage of Meghalaya through its well-maintained museum and exhibits.
  • > Nohsngithiang Falls (Seven Sisters Falls) – One of Meghalaya’s most iconic waterfalls, featuring seven parallel streams cascading down a massive limestone cliff, especially spectacular during the monsoon season.
  • > Eco Park – A beautifully landscaped park offering panoramic views of the surrounding valleys, waterfalls, and the lush green hills of Cherrapunji.
  • > Nohkalikai Falls – India’s tallest plunge waterfall, dropping dramatically from a height of over 1,100 feet amidst breathtaking cliffs and emerald-green surroundings.
  • > Mawsmai Cave – A fascinating limestone cave known for its natural rock formations, narrow passages, and easy accessibility for visitors.
  • > Arwah Cave (fossils & limestone formations) – A unique cave system famous for its ancient marine fossils, limestone formations, and underground chambers that offer an exciting exploration experience.
  • > Dainthlen Falls (subject to open) – A scenic waterfall associated with local Khasi folklore, surrounded by rugged rock formations and picturesque landscapes.
  • > Wah Kaba Falls (subject to open) – A stunning multi-tiered waterfall located near Cherrapunji, offering magnificent views of deep gorges and lush valleys.
  • > Wei Sawdong Falls (subject to open; requires short trek) - One of Meghalaya’s most beautiful hidden waterfalls, known for its crystal-clear turquoise pools and three-tiered cascades, accessible via a short trek.
  • • Overnight stay: Cherrapunjee
  • • After breakfast, proceed to:
    > Dawki River (Umngot River) – Famous for its crystal-clear waters, the Umngot River in Dawki offers a magical boating experience where boats appear to float on glass-like water.
    > Mawlynnong Village – Known as Asia’s Cleanest Village, Mawlynnong is celebrated for its cleanliness, eco-friendly lifestyle, beautiful gardens, and traditional Khasi culture.
    > Riwai Living Root Bridge – A A remarkable natural bridge created from the living roots of rubber trees, showcasing the unique bioengineering skills of the Khasi tribes.
    > Indo-Bangladesh Border Viewpoint – A scenic viewpoint offering breathtaking views of the international border, lush green plains of Bangladesh, and the surrounding hills of Meghalaya.
  • • Drive back to Shillong in the evening.
  • • Overnight stay: Shillong
  • • After breakfast, start your Shillong sightseeing tour and visit:
    > Elephant Falls - A stunning cascade of three successive falls, surrounded by lush greenery and offering spectacular views.
    > Shillong Peak - The highest point in Shillong, offering panoramic views of the city and the surrounding hills.
    > Ward's Lake - A beautiful artificial lake surrounded by gardens, offering boating and picnicking opportunities.
    > Don Bosco Centre for Indigenous Cultures - A museum showcasing the rich cultural heritage of the indigenous tribes of Northeast India.
    > Cathedral of Mary Help of Christians - A majestic architectural marvel, this beautiful church is one of the most iconic landmarks in Shillong, featuring stunning stained-glass windows and intricate Gothic-style architecture.
    > Lady Hydari Park - A charming park known for its beautiful flowerbeds, Japanese-style gardens, and a mini zoo housing various species of birds and reptiles.
  • • Overnight stay: Shillong
  • • Enjoy an early morning Jeep Safari in Kaziranga National Park, where you can witness the park's diverse wildlife, including the famous one-horned rhinoceros, elephants, tigers, deer, and various species of birds.
  • • Return to your hotel for breakfast.
  • • In the afternoon, enjoy another Jeep Safari in a different range of the park, exploring different habitats and increasing your chances of spotting more wildlife.
  • • Overnight stay: Kaziranga (Borgos Resort)
  • • Early morning Jeep Safari (included) at Kaziranga National Park – famous for the One-Horned Rhinoceros, swamp deer, elephants, wild water buffalo, and migratory birds.
  • • Post safari, drive back to Guwahati.
  • • Overnight Stay: Guwahati.
  • • Early morning visit to the revered Maa Kamakhya Temple, one of the most sacred Shakti Peethas in India.
  • • Return to your hotel for breakfast.
  • • Post darshan, transfer to Guwahati Airport / Railway Station for onward journey.

Other Information

Must Carry

  • Authentic Government ID Card
  • Comfortable clothing suitable for varying weather conditions
  • Light jacket or sweatshirt for Shillong and Cherrapunjee evenings
  • Sunglasses with UV protection
  • Sunscreen (SPF 30+) and lip balm
  • Personal medicines and basic first-aid kit
  • Mobile charger and power bank
  • Reusable water bottle
  • Umbrella or raincoat (rainfall is common in Meghalaya)
  • Cash for local shopping and remote areas
Best Time to Visit:
  • October to April is the ideal time to explore Shillong, Cherrapunjee, Kaziranga, and Guwahati.
  • November to February offers cool weather, clear skies, and excellent conditions for sightseeing and wildlife safaris.
  • March to April brings pleasant temperatures, blooming landscapes, and lush greenery across Meghalaya and Assam.
  • Kaziranga National Park remains open from November to April, making it the best period for wildlife spotting and jeep safaris.
  • The monsoon season (June to September) transforms Cherrapunjee into a paradise of waterfalls and greenery, though heavy rainfall may affect travel plans and outdoor activities.
  • For trekking and outdoor activities, the dry months from October to April are recommended.
Connectivity:
  • Mobile connectivity is generally good in Shillong and Guwahati, with major network providers offering reliable coverage.
  • In Cherrapunjee and remote areas, mobile signals may be weak or unavailable.
  • Public Wi-Fi is available in select cafes and hotels in Shillong and Guwahati.
  • Travelers are advised to download offline maps and carry a power bank for convenience.
  • ATMs and internet cafes are available in Shillong and Guwahati but may be limited in remote locations.
  • Carry sufficient cash as online payment facilities may not be available everywhere, especially in rural areas.
Weather:
  • Meghalaya's weather is highly unpredictable, with rainfall possible even during the dry season
  • Pack lightweight, waterproof clothing and an umbrella or raincoat for unexpected showers
  • Shillong and Cherrapunjee remain cool throughout the year
  • Kaziranga and Guwahati are generally warmer and more humid.
  • Carry a light jacket or sweatshirt for evenings, especially in the uplands
  • Sun protection (sunglasses, sunscreen) is essential year-round due to the high altitude
  • Weather conditions can change rapidly, so always check the forecast before activities
Wildlife Experience:
  • Kaziranga National Park is home to the one-horned rhinoceros, but sightings are not guaranteed
  • Jeep safaris and elephant safaris offer the best chances of spotting wildlife
  • Early morning safaris are most productive for birdwatching and wildlife viewing
  • Respect park rules and maintain silence during safaris to avoid disturbing the animals
  • Do not feed or touch any wild animals
  • Binoculars can enhance the wildlife viewing experience
Eco Tips:
  • Respect the local culture and traditions of Meghalaya
  • Avoid littering
  • Avoid using plastic bottles; use reusable water bottles instead
  • Support local communities by purchasing handicrafts and souvenirs directly from artisans
  • Respect wildlife and avoid disturbing animals in their natural habitat
  • Help preserve the natural beauty of Meghalaya and Assam
