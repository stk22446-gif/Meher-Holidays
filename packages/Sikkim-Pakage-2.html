Why Whispers of Kanchenjunga?

The Whispers of Kanchenjunga tour is a mesmerizing journey through the breathtaking landscapes of Darjeeling, Pelling, Gangtok, and Nathula Pass. Experience panoramic Himalayan views, serene monasteries, lush valleys, and the rich culture of the Eastern Himalayas with Meher Holidays.


Discover the majestic beauty of Kanchenjunga on this specially curated tour.

Whispers of Kanchenjunga Itinerary

Cost Per Person - INR 28,999/- including GST

ITINERARY (6 Nights / 7 Days)

Contact Us Download Itinerary
  • • Arrive at Bagdogra Airport (IXB) or NJP Railway Station, where our representative will greet you and assist with your transfer to Darjeeling (Altitude 7,100 ft / 2,100 m).
  • • On arrival, check into your hotel surrounded by the serenity of the hills. Spend the afternoon/evening exploring:
    > Chowrasta & Darjeeling Mall (on your own): The Victorian heart of Darjeeling, lined with quaint shops, tea boutiques, handicraft stalls, and food joints offering momos & thukpa.
    > Enjoy a pony ride for kids or simply soak in the charm from a bench at the Mall.
  • • Overnight stay at Darjeeling.
  • • Early Morning (before breakfast)
    > Sunrise from Tiger Hill: Witness the majestic Kanchenjunga bathed in golden light from one of the highest points in the area.
    > Batasia Loop: A scenic viewpoint with a panoramic 360-degree view of Darjeeling town and the Himalayas, featuring a magnificent war memorial.
    > Ghum Monastery: Visit one of the oldest monasteries in the region, perched at a height of 8,000 ft, housing a serene 15-ft statue of Buddha and priceless Buddhist artifacts.
  • • After breakfast:
    > Japanese Peace Pagoda: A beautiful Buddhist monument offering stunning views and a peaceful atmosphere.
    > Japanese Temple: A peaceful sanctuary with traditional Japanese architecture and serene prayer halls.
    > Padmaja Naidu Himalayan Zoological Park: A renowned high-altitude zoo housing rare Himalayan wildlife, including the Red Panda, Snow Leopard, and Himalayan Wolf. (Closed on Thursdays.)
    > Himalayan Mountaineering Institute (HMI): A famous mountaineering institute and museum dedicated to the history of Himalayan expeditions and legendary climbers. (Closed on Sundays.)
    > Tenzing Rock: A natural rock formation offering a thrilling rock-climbing experience (rock climbing charges extra). It’s a popular spot for adventure lovers and offers panoramic views of the surrounding hills.
    > Rangeet Valley Ropeway: A scenic cable car ride offering breathtaking views of lush tea gardens, valleys, rivers, and the surrounding hills.
    > Darjeeling Tea Estate: Visit the world-famous tea gardens of Darjeeling, known for producing premium tea and offering stunning views of rolling green plantations.
  • • Evening free at leisure.
  • • Overnight stay at Darjeeling.
  • • After breakfast, check out and proceed to Pelling (Altitude 2,150 m), a serene town in West Sikkim known for breathtaking views of Mt. Kanchenjunga.
  • • On arrival, check into your hotel and relax amidst pristine surroundings. Evening free to enjoy a quiet walk or sip hot tea while admiring the Himalayan landscape.
  • • Overnight stay at Pelling.
  • • After breakfast, set out for a full-day Pelling tour covering:
    > Rimbi Waterfall: Admire the scenic beauty of the cascading waterfalls.
    > Kanchenjunga Waterfalls: Visit the majestic beauty of one of Sikkim's most impressive waterfalls.
    > Pelling Skywalk: Walk on the thrilling glass skywalk offering stunning 360-degree panoramic views of the Himalayas and surrounding valleys.
    > Khecheopalri Lake: Visit the sacred wish-fulfilling lake, considered one of the holiest Buddhist sites in Sikkim.
    • Pemayangtse Monastery: EOne of Sikkim’s oldest and most important monasteries, renowned for its rich Buddhist heritage and stunning architecture.
    • Rabdentse Ruins: Explore the historic ruins of the former capital of Sikkim, offering panoramic views of the Himalayas and surrounding valleys.
    • Sangachoeling Monastery:A peaceful hilltop monastery known for its spiritual ambiance, ancient Buddhist traditions, and panoramic mountain views.
    • Orange Garden:A beautiful orchard known for its vibrant orange trees and stunning views of the surrounding landscapes.
  • • Return to hotel in the evening.
  • • Overnight stay at Pelling.
  • • Post breakfast, drive to Gangtok (Altitude 5,550 ft / 1,675 m) via a scenic route. En route visit:
    > Ravangla Buddha Park: A beautiful spiritual park featuring a magnificent 130-foot statue of Lord Buddha, surrounded by landscaped gardens and breathtaking views of the Himalayan mountains.
    > Temi Tea Garden: Explore the serene and picturesque Temi Tea Garden, one of Sikkim’s most famous tea estates, offering stunning panoramic views of the surrounding landscapes.
  • • On arrival in Gangtok, check into your hotel. Evening free to stroll around MG Marg (on own) or relax with coffee on the balcony overlooking the hills.
  • • Overnight stay at Gangtok.
  • • After breakfast, head out for a full-day excursion (permits required):
    > Tsomgo Lake: A stunning glacial lake (Altitude 12,415 ft / 3,785 m), considered sacred by the locals. On the way, you can enjoy the scenic beauty of the mountains and the lush green landscapes.
    > New Baba Harbhajan Singh Mandir: A revered shrine dedicated to Baba Harbhajan Singh, an Indian Army soldier honored for his devotion and believed to continue protecting the region even today.
  • • Nathula Pass (Indo-China border; closed Mondays; subject to permits & weather; extra cost).
  • • Return to Gangtok in the evening.
  • • Overnight stay at Gangtok.
  • • After breakfast, check out and drive back to Bagdogra Airport (IXB) / NJP Railway Station for your onward journey.
  • • Tour concludes with happy memories of Sikkim!

Other Information

Must Carry

  • Authentic Government ID Card (mandatory for permits and Nathula Pass)
  • Warm jacket or fleece jacket
  • Sunglasses with UV protection
  • Sunscreen (SPF 30+) and lip balm
  • Personal medicines and basic first-aid kit
  • Mobile charger and power bank
  • Reusable water bottle
  • Cash for local shopping and remote areas
  • Raincoat or umbrella (weather can change suddenly in the hills)
Best Time to Visit:
  • March to June is the ideal time to explore Darjeeling, Pelling, Gangtok, and Nathula Pass, with pleasant weather and clear mountain views.
  • October to December offers crisp skies and excellent visibility of the Kanchenjunga range, making it perfect for sightseeing and photography.
  • April and May are especially popular for blooming rhododendrons, lush landscapes, and comfortable temperatures.
  • December to February is ideal for travelers seeking snowfall and winter experiences, particularly around Nathula Pass.
  • The monsoon season (July to September) brings lush greenery and scenic beauty, though heavy rainfall may occasionally affect travel plans due to landslides and road closures.
Connectivity:
  • Mobile network coverage may be limited in remote areas.
  • Internet and Wi-Fi are available in most hotels and guesthouses, but connectivity can be unreliable in certain locations.
  • Jio, Airtel, BSNL, and Vi networks work well in Darjeeling, Pelling, and Gangtok.
  • Connectivity may be limited near Nathula Pass and other high-altitude areas.
Weather:
  • Nathula Pass is extremely cold throughout the year, with temperatures often remaining below freezing point.
  • Darjeeling, Pelling, and Gangtok remain cool throughout the year.
  • The region experiences heavy rainfall during the monsoon season (July to September), which may cause landslides and road closures.
  • Warm clothing is recommended in all seasons.
Permit Information:
  • Foreign and Indian nationals require Inner Line Permits (ILP) to visit Sikkim.
  • ILPs can be obtained from the Sikkim Tourism Information Centre in Siliguri, as well as through online portals and tour operators.
  • Special permits are required for Nathula Pass and may be obtained from the Sikkim Tourism & Civil Aviation Department in Gangtok.
  • Foreign nationals are not permitted to visit Nathula Pass.
Eco Tips:
  • Avoid using plastic bottles and carry reusable water bottles instead.
  • Dispose of waste properly and use designated bins for recycling and waste management.
  • Respect local customs and traditions, and dress modestly when visiting religious sites.
  • Avoid littering and polluting natural water sources, and use biodegradable products whenever possible.
  • Help preserve the pristine beauty of the Eastern Himalayas.
