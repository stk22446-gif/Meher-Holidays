Frequently Asked Questions

Your right to Know!

Q: How much does a Meghalaya trip cost?

A: Meghalaya trip costs vary based on travel style. Budget trips (4-5 days) cost around ₹22,000 per person, while mid-range packages can range from ₹30,000–₹40,000+.

Q: How to plan a Meghalaya trip for 5 days?

A: For a 5-day plan, spend 2 nights in Shillong, 2 in Cherrapunjee, and 1 exploring Mawlynnong and Dawki.

Q: Is Meghalaya safe for solo travellers?

A: Yes, Meghalaya is quite safe for solo and female travelers. Locals are friendly and crime is low.

Q: What should I pack for my visit to Meghalaya?

A: Carry light layers, a rain jacket, sturdy walking shoes, quick-dry clothes, and waterproof bags.
