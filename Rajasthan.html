Frequently Asked Questions

Your right to Know!

Q: What is the best time for a Rajasthan trip?

A: Winter (October to March) is the most pleasant time to explore the forts and deserts.

Q: How many days are enough for Rajasthan?

A: A comprehensive tour takes 10-14 days, but a quick trip to 2-3 cities can be done in 6-7 days.

Q: What are the must-visit cities?

A: Jaipur (Pink City), Udaipur (City of Lakes), Jodhpur (Blue City), and Jaisalmer (Golden City) are musts.

Q: Is Rajasthan safe for tourists?

A: Yes, it is very safe. Tourism is a major industry and locals are generally very helpful and hospitable.
