Frequently Asked Questions

Your right to Know!

Q: Is it safe to travel to Kashmir now?

A: Yes, Kashmir is a major tourist hub and very safe. Millions of tourists visit every year for its beauty.

Q: When can I see the Tulip Garden?

A: The Indira Gandhi Memorial Tulip Garden in Srinagar is typically open for 3-4 weeks in April.

Q: Does mobile internet work in Kashmir?

A: Only postpaid mobile connections work in J&K. Prepaid cards from other states will not have network access.

Q: Can I stay in a Houseboat on Dal Lake?

A: Absolutely! Staying in a traditional cedar-wood houseboat is one of the most iconic experiences in Srinagar.

Q: What is the best time for snow in Gulmarg?

A: For heavy snow and skiing, the best time is from late December to late February.
