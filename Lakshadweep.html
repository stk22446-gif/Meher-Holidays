Frequently Asked Questions

Your right to Know!

Q: Do I need a permit to visit Lakshadweep?

A: Yes, an Entry Permit is mandatory for all visitors. We handle the permit process for our guests.

Q: How to reach Lakshadweep?

A: You can take a flight from Kochi to Agatti or travel by passenger ships from Kochi.

Q: Which islands are open for tourists?

A: Agatti, Bangaram, Kadmat, and Kavaratti are the most popular islands open for tourists.

Q: Are water sports available?

A: Yes, Lakshadweep is a paradise for Scuba Diving, Snorkeling, and Kayaking.
